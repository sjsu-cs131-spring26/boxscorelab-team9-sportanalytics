# Meeting 2: Decision Review and Finalize Brief
Team: The Box Score Lab
Date/Time: 2026-03-09 10:00
Duration: 50 minutes
Facilitator (PM): Oliver Majano
Notetaker: Minn Naung

Goal of the meeting:
Review evidence artifacts, agree on a recommendation, and finalize the Decision Brief and Action Plan.

1) Status check (5 min)
- What artifacts are complete?
- What is blocked?

2) Evidence walkthrough (20 to 25 min)
For each artifact:
- What does it show (one sentence)?
- Why does it matter to the decision?
- Any caveats?

Artifacts:
- A1: Correlation heatmap - shows how the relationship between each stat and winning has shifted from the 1970s through 2020s.
- A2: Three-point revolution trend chart - tracks the rise of three-point volume, top-quartile win rates, and correlation with winning over time.
- A3: Top-quartile win rate comparison table - compares how often top-25% teams in each stat actually win, by decade.
- Trust check: FG% consistency verification - confirms FG% holds as the strongest predictor in every individual decade.
- Assumption test: 3PA vs. 3PM correlation split - confirms that three-point accuracy, not volume, drives the correlation with winning in the 2020s.

3) Recommendation drafting (10 to 15 min)
Draft 1 to 3 recommendation bullets and verify they are supported by evidence.

4) Action plan finalization (10 min)
Confirm owners, due dates, and definitions of done for remaining deliverables (Decision Brief, final visualizations, presentation).

5) Wrap (2 min)
Confirm the plan to finalize the Decision Brief and the remaining tasks.
